## Full description

Make every move count in Blokki Pop, a colorful block-placement puzzle.

Drag shapes onto the board, complete rows and columns, and watch them clear in a burst of color. Plan ahead, make space for the next pieces, and build satisfying combo chains as the challenge gradually grows.

• Easy to pick up, satisfying to master
• Colorful blocks and lively clear effects
• Plan each placement to keep the board open
• Chase a new personal best

How long can you keep the board clear? Place your next block and find out.
